Privacy Policy — Torque Trails

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Torque Trails is developed and published by Rillwood Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

We have no servers and we collect nothing about you at all. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, and no adverts. This version of the game contains no advertising software, so nothing about you or your device is collected, shared or sent anywhere by us or by anyone else.

The only information the app handles is your saved progress, and it never leaves your phone.

If adverts are ever added in a future version, they will be optional rewarded video only — a short advert you may choose to watch to double the coins from a run, never a banner and never between levels. That is described under "Advertising" below, and this policy and the app's Google Play data-safety declaration will both be updated before any such version ships.

What the app stores on your device

The game saves your progress locally so you can close it and come back. This data never leaves your phone and we cannot see it:

  • Coins, gems and which cars you own
  • Stars and best distances per level
  • Upgrades, paint, wheels and tyres
  • Audio volume settings and the last level you played
  • Which in-app purchases you own, so the game can honour them offline

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions the app requests

  • Modify audio settings — lets the engine sound and effects mix correctly. It does not give the app access to your microphone, your media library or any recordings.
  • Vibrate — the short buzz on a landing or a pickup.
  • Internet / network state — used only to reach Google Play when you open the shop or make a purchase. The game itself never sends anything anywhere.

The app does not request the microphone, camera, contacts, location, files, storage, or any other sensitive permission. This is enforced at build time: the permissions above are the complete list in the published app bundle.

Advertising

The current version of Torque Trails contains no advertising software and shows no adverts of any kind. It ships without an ads SDK, so nothing described in this section happens today, and no advertising identifier leaves your device. The rest of this section describes what will apply if adverts are added in a future version — at which point this policy will be updated, the Google Play data-safety declaration will be changed accordingly, and the store listing will state that the app contains ads.

When adverts are enabled, Torque Trails will show rewarded video adverts only, and only when you choose to watch one. They will be provided by Google AdMob.

To serve an advert, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier you control in Android Settings)
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • app interaction and diagnostic information relating to the advert

Google uses this for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not receive any of it. We are paid an aggregate amount based on adverts watched; we cannot see who watched them.

Your choices:

  • Consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before any personalised advert is served. You can change your answer at any time from the privacy options link in the game's settings.
  • Opt out of personalisation anywhere. Android Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID. The game continues to work exactly the same; adverts simply become non-personalised.
  • Ignore adverts entirely. They are opt-in. Never tapping "watch ad" costs you nothing except the optional bonus coins.

Advert content. We have configured our AdMob account to request only general-audience ("G"-rated) adverts and to block gambling, alcohol, dating, sexual, drug-related and get-rich-quick categories. Google states that this filtering is automatic and not guaranteed to catch every advert. If you see something that should not be there, email us and we will block it directly.

Purchases

Torque Trails offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, gem packs and a starter pack). These are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your name, address, card number or any other payment detail — Google handles the transaction and only tells the app whether a purchase succeeded, so the game can grant the item.

Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Torque Trails is rated for general audiences but is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone of any age. Adverts are requested with child-directed treatment explicitly disabled, in line with the app's 13+ target audience.

Network use

The app does not require an internet connection to play, and every level is fully playable offline. It uses the network for exactly one thing: contacting Google Play when you open the shop or make a purchase. There is no other network traffic of any kind — no analytics call, no crash report, no advert request.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent data, and we hold none. In this version nothing about you is collected by us or by any third party, so there is nothing to share. (Should adverts be added later, the data Google would collect for them is listed under "Advertising" above.)

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Removing the app from your device removes the local save entirely.

For the advertising data Google collects, use Google's own controls: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, adjust your consent choices via the privacy options link in the game's settings, or use Google's My Activity.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, and the current version will always be available at this URL.
