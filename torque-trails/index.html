Torque Trails

Climb, jump and land. A physics driving game with 120 hand-built levels, where every hill is simulated, not scripted.

Screenshots

What’s inside

Easy to start. Hard to master.

120

Hand-built levels

Grass, desert highway, snow, mud, sand, rock and the moon. Every crest, gap and landing placed on purpose.

PHYSICS

Real weight and grip

From a little hatchback to a bus to a hypercar, each with its own suspension, traction and engine.

UPGRADES

Upgrade and tune

Spend what you earn on engine, suspension and tyres, and pick compounds for mud, ice or rock.

FUEL

Fuel is the clock

Fuel drains as you drive and cans are spaced along the course. Dawdle and you walk.

TRICKS

Tricks pay

Backflips, front flips and long airtime earn coins, with a combo for clean landings.

OFFLINE

Play offline

No account, no sign-in, no internet needed. Your progress is saved on your device.
