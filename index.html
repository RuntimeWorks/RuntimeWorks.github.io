How we work

Small company. Clear commitments.

Three rules apply to everything we make, whether it is used in a commercial kitchen or on a phone at home.

01

One product, one job

Each product solves one problem for one kind of user, and stays focused on it instead of growing features nobody asked for.

02

Built and supported locally

Designed, built and supported from Charlottetown. When you contact us, you reach the people who make the product.

03

Your data stays yours

We collect only what a product needs to work. Business records can be exported at any time, and every app has its own privacy policy.
