Sound familiar?

Paper logs fail in the same four places.

None of this is about caring less. A clipboard on a busy shift is easy to skip, easy to backfill and hard to find later. That’s what inspectors notice.

BeforeThe clipboard

  • Readings filled in at the end of the shift, in the same pen, by one person.
  • Missing pages in the binder on inspection day.
  • A bad reading is written down, but nothing says what was done about it.
  • New staff don’t know which checks to do, or when.

AfterBoldsand logbook

  • Each check is timestamped and initialled by whoever did it, on their own phone.
  • Every record lives in one place, searchable, and prints as a clean report.
  • Out-of-range readings ask for the fix and a recheck before the check can close.
  • Reminders tell the shift what’s due, so nobody has to remember.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do my staff need to install anything?

No. The logbook opens on any phone, tablet or computer. Staff tap a link, do the check and initial it. We show everyone how on day one.

What does it cost?

The records check is free. For founding businesses, we agree on a monthly price with you before you pay anything, and that price stays the same as long as you stay. No long contract during the pilot.

Does this replace my food safety plan or my inspector?

No. Your plan and your inspector decide what needs to be checked. The logbook is where your team records that the checks were done, what the readings were, and what was done when something was off.

What happens to my records?

They belong to you. You can export or print all of them at any time, and if you leave, you take them with you. We only use them to run your logbook.

We already use paper and it works. Why change?

If your binder is complete, on time and easy to find, keep it. Most aren’t, and the free check will show you either way in 20 minutes.

Do you work outside PEI?

Yes. On site in PEI, and by video anywhere in Atlantic Canada. Other provinces are welcome to get in touch.
