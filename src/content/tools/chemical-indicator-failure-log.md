This free printable log is for dental offices in the United States to record each failed chemical or mechanical indicator and the reprocessing that followed. When a mechanical reading or a chemical indicator shows inadequate processing, the instrument packs in that load should not be used until they have been reprocessed <cite>(CDC, 2003 dental infection-control guidelines)</cite>.

## What the sources say

Each line below ends with its source in plain words.

-   Monitor each load with mechanical readings, for example time, temperature and pressure, and with chemical indicators <cite>(CDC, 2003 dental infection-control guidelines)</cite>.
    
-   Mechanical monitoring involves checking the sterilizer gauges, computer displays or printouts, and documenting the sterilization pressure, temperature and exposure time in your sterilization records <cite>(CDC, Summary of Infection Prevention Practices in Dental Settings)</cite>.
    
-   A chemical indicator should be used inside every package, to verify that the sterilizing agent, for example steam, has penetrated the package and reached the instruments inside <cite>(CDC, Summary of Infection Prevention Practices in Dental Settings)</cite>.
    
-   If the internal indicator is not visible from the outside, an exterior chemical indicator is also used on the package; if the internal chemical indicator is visible, an external indicator is not needed <cite>(CDC, 2003 dental infection-control guidelines; CDC, Infection Prevention Checklist for Dental Settings; CDC, Guideline for Disinfection and Sterilization in Healthcare Facilities, 2008)</cite>.
    
-   Instrument packs are not used when the mechanical or the chemical indicators show inadequate processing; the items in the load are reprocessed before they are used <cite>(CDC, 2003 dental infection-control guidelines; CDC, Infection Prevention Checklist for Dental Settings)</cite>.
    

## The printable log

Fill it in on screen and then press Print, or print it blank and write it in by hand. Nothing you type on this page is stored or sent anywhere.

Prints on US Letter paper, landscape.

## How to use it

Four steps, from a blank page to a signed row for every failure.

1.  ### Fill in the header
    
    Office name, the sterilizer's label and the month or year the sheet covers.
    
2.  ### One row for each failure
    
    Write the date, the sterilizer and the load number, then mark which indicator failed: the internal one, the external one, or the mechanical reading.
    
3.  ### Record the packs
    
    How many packs were affected, that they were held, and the load number they were reprocessed as.
    
4.  ### Cause and initials
    
    Write the cause found, then close the row with your initials.
