## Where the source rows disagree

Three disagreements run through the source pack.

### How often the air-removal test runs

Row C03-13 says “periodically … as recommended by the manufacturer”; row D08-4 says the test “must be performed daily in an empty dynamic-air-removal sterilizer” (CDC, 2003 dental and 2008 sterilization guidelines). Dental Logbook will run it on each day of use, before the first load.

### Empty or loaded chamber when requalifying

Row D08-5 runs “three consecutive empty steam cycles”; row FAQ-6 re-challenges “with spore tests in three consecutive fully loaded chamber sterilization cycles” (CDC, 2008 disinfection and sterilization guideline; CDC, sterilization monitoring page for dental settings, 2024). Dental Logbook records the chamber state of each cycle and does not choose for the office.

### Weekly, or counted in use hours

Row C03-1 says “at least weekly”; row FL-2 for dry heat and ethylene oxide sterilizers says “every 120 hours of operation at sterilization parameters or every thirty days, whichever comes first” (CDC, 2003 dental guidelines; Florida rule 64B5-25.003). Dental Logbook will set the spore test due at the earliest of the dates these rows imply.

## What to record

A good load record holds one row per cycle: date, start time, sterilizer, cycle type, load number, contents, wrapped or unwrapped, implantable device yes or no, temperature, pressure and exposure time checked against the manufacturer’s parameters, cycle minutes, indicator results, the operator’s initials, and the outcome — released, held for the spore test, or failed and reprocessed. A good spore test record adds the indicator’s lot and its control. No form here needs a patient’s name; the load number on the pack does the tying.
