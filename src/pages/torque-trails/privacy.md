**Last updated: 7 October 2026**

Torque Trails (Android app `com.boldsand.torquetrails`) is made and published by **Boldsand Software Inc.** ("Boldsand", "we", "us").

## What this app collects

Nothing. The current version has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising software. We run no servers and receive no information about you or your device.

## What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress so you can close it and come back. It stays on your phone:

-   Coins, gems and which cars you own
-   Stars and best distance for each level
-   Upgrades, paint, wheels and tyres
-   Audio settings and the last level played
-   Which in-app purchases you own, so they work offline

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. We never see your name, address or card details — Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether it succeeded, so the item can be granted.

Google's handling of payment information is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Security

Save data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Because nothing is transmitted or stored outside your device, there is no account to break into and no server holding your data.

## How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your save data stays on your device until you remove it. **Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.** We hold no copy, so there is nothing to export, correct or erase on request.

Records of purchases are held by Google, not by us, and are covered by Google's policy above.

## Permissions the app requests

-   **Modify audio settings** — so engine sound and effects mix correctly. This does not give access to your microphone or recordings.
-   **Vibrate** — the short buzz on a landing or a pickup.
-   **Internet and network state** — used to reach Google Play for the shop and purchases.
-   **Google Play billing** — lets the shop ask Google Play to process a purchase.
-   **Keep the device awake (wake lock)** — requested by the audio libraries the game is built on, so sound is not cut off during play. It gives no access to your data.

The app does not request the microphone, camera, contacts, location, files or storage.

## Future versions

Features may be added later, including advertising. Any version that collects or shares information will describe it here before that version is released, and the app's Google Play data safety declaration and store listing will be updated to match.

The "Last updated" date above changes whenever this policy does. The current version is always available at this URL.
